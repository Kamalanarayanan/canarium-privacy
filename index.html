Canarium Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Canarium is a macOS app operated by CRIT Studio. It gives AI tools access to a working copy of a folder you choose and lets you review changes before applying them to your original files.

What Canarium processes

Canarium processes the files you select for a task, the instructions you provide, app settings and task activity. Task records can include plans, tool calls and their inputs, approvals, timing and model usage. Canarium keeps its working copies and task logs on your Mac. The local gateway keeps configured service API keys in macOS Keychain.

When information leaves your Mac

If you choose a cloud AI provider or connect a chat app such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, that service may receive your prompts, task context and file contents that the AI reads for the task. If you use an online tool, such as a design asset search or Figma, Canarium sends the query or identifier needed to fulfil the request to that provider. Those services handle the data they receive under their own terms and privacy policies.

Canarium’s “Keep this private” option runs helper models on your Mac. If your main AI is a cloud service, that service may still process the material it reads. Your choice of main AI determines where that part of the task is processed.

Planned Pinterest integration

Canarium is applying for access to the official Pinterest API. If approved, the initial integration is intended to let you use Pinterest OAuth to voluntarily connect your account and view Pins and boards from that authenticated account as design references. Canarium will request the permissions needed for this feature. The connection will be optional, and you will sign in directly with Pinterest. Canarium will not ask for your Pinterest password or session cookies.

The planned integration will fetch the requested results for temporary display and link back to Pinterest. Pinterest API response content will be excluded from saved project files, persistent logs and AI prompts, and will not be shared with model providers, sold, used for advertising or used to train AI. Canarium will not publish or modify Pins or boards through this integration. You can revoke Canarium’s access through your Pinterest account settings. Pinterest handles its own sign in and service activity under the Pinterest Privacy Policy.

Retention and your choices

Local task files and logs can remain on your Mac after a session ends. You can reject a task’s changes, remove local copies and records you no longer need, and remove configured credentials from macOS Keychain. Changes you choose to keep become part of your original folder. For information already sent to a connected service, use that service’s privacy and deletion controls.

If you contact CRIT Studio, we use the contact details and information you provide to respond and retain them as needed to resolve your request or meet legal obligations. You can ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information that CRIT Studio holds by emailing support@critstudios.com.
